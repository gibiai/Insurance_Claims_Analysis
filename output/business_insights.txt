BUSINESS INSIGHTS — Insurance Charges Analysis
==================================================

1. Smokers cost 3.8x more than non-smokers ($32,050 vs $8,441). A significant premium loading for smokers is justified.

2. Obese smokers are the highest-risk segment at $41,558 average charge: over 3x the portfolio average

3. Charges grow 95% from the youngest to oldest age band ($9,201 → $17,903). Age-based pricing tiers are warranted.

4. The Southeast region has the highest average charges ($14,735), suggesting mild regional risk adjustment.
